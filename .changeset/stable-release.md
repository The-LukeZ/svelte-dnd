First stable release of the Svelte 5 rewrite of `svelte-dnd-action`. Zones and drag handles are attachments (`dndzone`, `dragHandleZone`, `dragHandle`), updates arrive through the `onconsider` and `onfinalize` options, and global settings go through `configure`.

Read the [documentation](https://svelte-dnd.thelukez.com) to get started, or the [migration guide](https://svelte-dnd.thelukez.com/docs/migrating) to move from `svelte-dnd-action`. See `1.0.0-next.0` below for the full list of changes since the fork.
